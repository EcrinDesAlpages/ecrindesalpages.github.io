La Plagne - Paradiski

An exceptional, vast ski area offering a wide range of activities

Linked to Les Arcs and Peisey-Vallandry, La Plagne is part of the prestigious Paradiski ski area, offering 425 km of slopes suited to all levels, from beginner to expert. With 70% of the area above 2,000 metres, the resort enjoys quality snow cover throughout the season, from winter into spring — all set against a spectacular backdrop where alpine panoramas make every run unforgettable.

Making the most of your stay

Shops

In the heart of the resort, enjoy a wide range of shops and services within walking distance: bakery, SPAR mini-markets, sports shops, as well as cheesemongers and delicatessens where you can discover Savoyard specialities.

Activities

After skiing, extend the day however you like, just steps from the apartment. Bowling, cinema, the Magic Pool water park, spa, dog-sledding or snowmobile outings... Whether with family or friends, enjoy a wide range of experiences to create unforgettable memories.

For children

Flocons et Petits Pas nursery: the municipal nursery welcomes children from 5 months to 4 years.
French Ski School (ESF): located just steps from the accommodation, offering lessons for all levels so children can learn to ski safely.
