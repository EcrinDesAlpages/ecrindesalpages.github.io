Rental terms

  • Tourist tax: included in the price shown.
  • Stay: weekly rental only, Sunday to Sunday. Key handover from 4pm (please notify the concierge for a late arrival). Check-out by 10am on Sunday morning.
  • Payment: 40% non-refundable deposit, balance due 35 days before arrival.
  • Security deposit: €600, to be paid at the latest with the balance. Deposit payable by bank transfer or uncashed cheques (€500 for the apartment, €100 for cleaning). Once checked by the concierge, we will refund by bank transfer or destroy the cheques within 10 days at the latest.
  • Cleaning: not included in the price shown. Cleaning is the tenant's responsibility, or can be requested at booking for a €100 supplement. If end-of-stay cleaning is not carried out, a €100 deduction will be made from the deposit.
  • Not included: bed linen, bath linen.
  • No pets allowed.
  • Non-smoking apartment
  • The accommodation is not adapted for people with reduced mobility.
  • Skis, snowboards and ski/snowboard boots are not allowed inside the apartment or on the balcony. Please use the ski locker provided on the ground floor of the residence.
  • You will receive an email from the concierge a few days before your arrival with instructions for collecting the keys. You will also receive an email with your welcome booklet and the apartment inventory.
